01 — The object

Not a picture
of a thing

Everything above this line is being drawn right now, on your machine, about sixty times a second.

Bloom of Remembrance is not an image file. The figure is lathed geometry with a hand-written gild on it. The vine on its chest is a mask lit from the root up, like a fuse. The field it stands in is a few hundred instanced quads carrying a tint that runs from highlight through gold to shadow, because a field of highlights alone reads as fog.

It answers you. The lens follows your cursor on a spring; the body warms as you close on it; touch it and the field throws petals and the vine relights. None of that is decoration — it is the argument. A brand can be a fixed asset, or it can be a system that behaves.

We build the second kind.

02 — Practice

Four things
we make

Briefs combine them; each stands alone. Every engagement ends in something you own outright — files, source, and the reasoning behind both.

01

Identity systems

Marks, type, colour and the rules that keep them honest under pressure. Built as tokens first, so the system survives contact with real engineering instead of living in a PDF.

  • Wordmark and symbol, drawn not generated
  • Type and colour as CSS custom properties
  • A written standard, short enough to be read

02

Generative artwork

Images that are programs. Shaders, geometry, procedural motifs — work that recolours with a theme, reframes on a phone, and never ships as a flat asset that dates the moment it is exported.

  • WebGL and canvas, authored as source
  • Deterministic from a seed, so nothing drifts
  • A still fallback for every live piece

03

Interface design

Screens designed against the constraints they will actually meet: real copy, real data, real latency, real accessibility requirements. Drawn in the browser, because that is where it has to work.

  • Layout, hierarchy and states in full
  • Keyboard and screen-reader paths, not retrofitted
  • Handover as working front-end code

04

Motion

Timing as a material. Springs for anything a hand drives, tweens for anything the page drives, and a reduced-motion path that is a considered still rather than an absence.

  • One motion vocabulary across DOM and canvas
  • Durations and curves as shared tokens
  • Measured, not guessed, on mid-range hardware

04 — Studio

The design arm
of a futures practice

WATL makes the visual work for Wattle Technologies, and takes outside briefs when the problem is interesting.

Wattle Technologies is a modern futurism practice: signal intelligence, scenario architecture, applied prototypes. That work produces a great deal that has to be looked at — scenarios that need a form, prototypes that need an interface, arguments that need an image before anyone will fund them.

WATL is the arm that does the looking-at. It means our design work starts from an unusual place: not a moodboard, but a claim about the future that somebody has to be persuaded is real.

Acacia pycnantha flowers while the ground is still cold — the first colour in the paddock, and the last thing you would bet on. That is the reference the studio is named for, and it is also the brief.
