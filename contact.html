Good briefs tend to

  • Name a decision that is genuinely still open
  • Sit two to six years out, not two quarters
  • Have someone senior who will act on the answer
  • Include the thing you are afraid the answer will be

We will say no to

  • Work where the decision is already made
  • Trend decks with no falsifiable claim required
  • Anything priced by the hour

steps

No procurement theatre. If the fit is wrong we tell you in the first call, and where we can we point you at whoever is right.

01

Reply in two days

A real response from whoever would run the work, not an account manager. Usually with two or three questions attached.

02

Forty-five minutes

One call to establish whether the decision is open, who owns it, and which engagement shape fits. Free, and occasionally enough on its own.

03

Fixed scope, fixed fee

A one-page statement of work with the artefacts named and dated. If we are booked out, you get a start date rather than a junior team.
